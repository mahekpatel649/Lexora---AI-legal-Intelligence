# ⚖️ Lexora — AI Legal Intelligence Platform

**AI-powered legal document intelligence and case management for India.**

Lexora is a legal-tech web application prototype designed to help lawyers and law firms simplify legal document analysis, organize cases, and access AI-assisted legal information.

The platform focuses on reducing repetitive legal work through document summarization, case organization, and AI-powered question answering with an Indian legal context.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Legal professionals often work with lengthy legal documents, multiple case files, contracts, and important deadlines.

Lexora aims to make these tasks easier through a centralized platform that combines AI-powered document intelligence with practical case management tools.

### Key Objectives

- Simplify legal document analysis.
- Extract important information from legal documents.
- Generate structured document summaries.
- Assist with case-related questions.
- Organize legal cases and associated documents.
- Support legal research focused on Indian laws.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. Dashboard
- Overview of active cases.
- Total uploaded documents.
- Upcoming hearing dates.
- Recent case activity.

### 2. AI Legal Document Intelligence
- Upload legal documents.
- Generate AI-assisted document summaries.
- Identify important facts and dates.
- Highlight potential risks and unclear clauses.
- Extract relevant legal information.

### 3. AI Legal Assistant
- Ask questions about uploaded case documents.
- Get contextual AI-generated responses.
- Assist with understanding legal terminology.
- Support document-based question answering.
- Provide source references where available.

### 4. Case Management
- Create and organize legal cases.
- Maintain client and case information.
- Track case status.
- Record filing and hearing dates.
- Associate documents with cases.

### 5. Document Management
- Organize uploaded legal documents.
- Associate documents with specific cases.
- Search and review document records.
- Access document analysis results.

## 🇮🇳 Indian Legal Context

Lexora is designed with Indian legal workflows in mind.

Its intended legal research coverage includes:

| Legal Area | Relevant Legislation |
|---|---|
| Criminal Law | Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023 |
| Criminal Procedure | Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023 |
| Evidence Law | Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 |
| Constitutional Law | Constitution of India |
| Contract Law | Indian Contract Act, 1872 |
| Corporate Law | Companies Act, 2013 |
| Consumer Law | Consumer Protection Act, 2019 |
| Cyber Law | Information Technology Act, 2000 |

**Official legal resources:**
- [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/)
- [Supreme Court of India](https://www.sci.gov.in/)
- [eCourts Services](https://ecourts.gov.in/)

Legal provisions and judgments should be verified against authoritative sources and applicable amendments.

## ⚙️ Setup and Deployment

Lexora is structured as a Hatchable project.

To deploy your own copy:

1. Download or clone the project repository.
2. Open [Hatchable](https://hatchable.com/deploy).
3. Import the project ZIP or GitHub repository.
4. Configure the required AI provider credentials through the platform's secure settings.
5. Deploy the application.
6. Open the generated application URL.

**Note:** AI functionality requires a valid, supported provider configuration. The project should not be considered fully operational until the AI connection and document-processing workflow have been tested.

## 🔐 Responsible AI and Privacy

Lexora is intended as an AI-assisted legal productivity tool.

- AI-generated information may contain errors.
- Legal outputs require professional verification.
- Uploaded legal documents may contain confidential information.
- API credentials must be stored securely.
- AI-generated legal references must not be treated as verified authorities without checking the original sources.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Lexora is a prototype developed for educational, learning, and hackathon purposes.

It does not replace qualified legal professionals, provide guaranteed legal advice, or independently establish the correctness of legal interpretations.

All AI-generated summaries, legal information, and analysis must be independently verified before professional or legal use.
